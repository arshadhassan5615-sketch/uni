## Pitfalls found so far (the video will state these honestly)

1. Different systems, different numbers. FAO counts deforestation (land-use change, country reports). GFW/UMD counts
   tree cover loss from satellite (includes fire and plantation harvest). INPE PRODES counts clear-cut of primary forest
   in the Brazilian Legal Amazon only. These must never be compared as if they measure the same thing.
2. PRODES "estimated" vs "consolidated" differ (5,796 vs 5,731 km2 for 2025). The video will say which is used.
3. I summed INPE's raw polygons and got 5,255 km2 for 2025, not 5,796. The official rate includes extra
   adjustments, so raw polygon sums must NOT be shown as "the rate". Maps will show the polygons; rates come only
   from INPE's published figures.
